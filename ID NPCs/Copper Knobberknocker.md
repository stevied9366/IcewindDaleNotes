CG Rock Gnome Acolyte of Lathander

A pessimistic rock gnome tinkerer

Rents the attic in the House of the Morninglord in [[Bryn Shander]], and begrudgingly helps with services and chores. 

Argues incessantly with [[Mishaan]]. 

Walks around in a fuzzy suit and hood that he made himself; although it's quite warm, it makes him look like a teddy bear.
