LG Shield Dwarf Commoner

Owner of Blackiron Blades in [[Bryn Shander]]

Brother to [[Elza]]

Manufactures the cheapest blades in Ten-Towns

Most of the veteran sellswords in Ten-Towns disdain Garn's smithcraft, due to the uninspiring quality of his work. Jokes told about hapless newcomers to Icewind Dale often end with the line "an' 'e was carrying a Blackiron blade, to boot!"
