Lonelywood

Population - 100

Leaders - Speaker Nimsy Huddle (LG Strongheart Halfling Commoner)

Militia - 50 Soldiers (tribal warriors) and 4 Veterans

Heraldry - Two narrow white triangles, one descending from the center top, the other rising from center bottom, almost meet on a forest-green field. 

Sacrifice to Auril - Food

Rivalries - Bremen, Targos, Termalaine

  

Ever since the local inn closed its doors, Nimsy has opened her heated attic to visitors looking for a place to crash for the night. She bakes halfling-shaped cookies and brings them to meetings of the Council of Speakers. 

  

The top triangle in the crest represents an icicle, denoting Lonelywood's northerly location; the bottom triangle is an ivory horn, representing the town's scrimshaw trade. 

  

Travel times (assuming on foot travel):

- Teraelaine - 2 Hours
